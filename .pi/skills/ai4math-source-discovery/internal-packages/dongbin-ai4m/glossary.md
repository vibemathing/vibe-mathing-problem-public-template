# Glossary

**Agent / 智能体** — A model-centered workflow that decomposes a task, uses tools or retrieval, observes feedback, and iterates. (Ch 1, Ch 2)

**AI4M (AI for Mathematics)** — AI methods used to reduce the time cost and increase the effectiveness of mathematical research. (Ch 1)

**AI Scientist** — Agentic systems that search, improve, or evaluate scientific/mathematical candidates, represented in the guide by AlphaEvolve-style work. (Ch 2)

**AlphaEvolve** — An evolve-style AI Scientist example highlighted for tasks with clear quantitative objectives and usable initial solutions. (Ch 2)

**autoformalization / 自动形式化** — AI-assisted translation from informal mathematical language into formal statements or proofs. (Ch 2)

**FATE / FATE-X** — High-difficulty algebra evaluation sets mentioned as examples of rigorous mathematical benchmarking. (Ch 2)

**formal verifier / 形式化验证器** — A machine checker that provides rigorous acceptance/error feedback for formal statements and proofs. (Ch 2)

**general model / 通用模型** — A reusable reasoning/formalization system intended to solve a class of mathematical tasks rather than one narrow research bottleneck. (Ch 2)

**insight & connection / 洞察与连接** — Discovering patterns, structural relations, analogies, and viewpoints that advance mathematical understanding. (Ch 1, Ch 3)

**knowledge navigation / 知识导航** — Finding and learning relevant theories, tools, definitions, related results, or formal-library facts. (Ch 1)

**Lean** — The formal language/ecosystem recommended in the guide for formal mathematics and machine-checked proofs. (Ch 2)

**LeanSearch** — Semantic search for Lean/mathlib, introduced by the author's team to reduce formal-library retrieval cost. (Ch 2)

**mathlib** — Lean's major community library of formalized mathematics. (Ch 2)

**mathematical digitization / 数学数字化** — Converting ordinary mathematical expression into a formal representation usable by machines. (Ch 2)

**proof & verification / 证明与验证** — Constructing proof steps, checking counterexamples, and establishing rigorous correctness. (Ch 1, Ch 2, Ch 3)

**reap** — A Lean tactic derived from REAL-Prover for automatically filling proof fragments. (Ch 2)

**REAL-Prover** — A Lean-oriented reasoning/proving system from the author's team, cited as part of the recent formal reasoning landscape. (Ch 2)

**special-purpose tool / 专用工具** — A targeted AI/ML tool built around a specific mathematical data bottleneck to expose useful structure or inspire conjectures. (Ch 2)

**verifier feedback / 验证器反馈** — Using formal checker success/errors as high-quality signals during proof search, model training, or inference. (Ch 2)
