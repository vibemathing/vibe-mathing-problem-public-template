# Workflow — Mathematical discovery / open problems

1. Formalize objective, constraints, and what counts as improvement.
2. Generate candidate programs/constructions/proofs using search or an agent workbench.
3. Evaluate candidates with a deterministic/domain-specific evaluator whenever possible.
4. Search adversarially for counterexamples and hidden assumptions.
5. Formalize high-value claims in a proof assistant when feasible.
6. Separate three judgments: correctness, novelty, significance.
7. Use expert audit for significance and context even when formal correctness is established.
8. Record compute/search budget and negative results to avoid false discovery narratives.
