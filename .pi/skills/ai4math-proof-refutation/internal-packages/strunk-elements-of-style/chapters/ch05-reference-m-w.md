## Quick decision rule

Chapter V's singular-`they`, split-infinitive, strict `shall/will`, and several lexical restrictions are especially time-sensitive. In modern editing, report their historical source status and follow the user's contemporary style unless they explicitly request strict Strunk or period imitation.
